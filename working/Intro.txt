Welcome to "Bible in a Year" with Scott Purcell.

I'm so glad you're joining me!

This is a daily reading of portions of the Bible, following a plan that will include the entire Bible in a year.
Each day will include three readings: one reading from the Old Testament, one from the New Testament, and one from Psalms or Proverbs.

This plan is sourced from Biblica.com and is designed to bring you the richness and depth of the Bible from Genesis to Revelation, while providing some variety and balance for each day.
