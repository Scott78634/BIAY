Glory Fills the House: The Completion of the Tabernacle & The Transfiguration (Day 57 / Feb 26)

What happens when God finally moves in? Today, we reach the climax of the book of Exodus as the Tabernacle is finished and the glory of the Lord fills the tent. This theme of divine glory continues in Mark, where Peter, James, and John witness Jesus transfigured in radiant light, revealing His true identity as the Son of God.

Today’s Scripture:

📖 Exodus 39–40 – The priestly garments are completed, the Tabernacle is assembled according to God's command, and the cloud of glory descends upon the sanctuary.

📖 Mark 9:2–32 – Jesus takes the inner circle up a high mountain for the Transfiguration, then descends to heal a boy with an unclean spirit and teach on His coming death.

📖 Psalm 26 – A prayer of David, asking for God’s vindication and declaring his desire to walk in integrity and bless the Lord in the congregation.
