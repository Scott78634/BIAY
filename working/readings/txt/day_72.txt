From the Wilderness to the Temple: Ending Leviticus & Beginning Luke (Day 72)

What happens when God’s silence is finally broken? Today, we conclude the book of Leviticus with a sobering look at the consequences of disobedience and the laws regarding vows and tithes. Then, we transition into the Gospel of Luke, where the "orderly account" begins not with a king, but with an elderly priest in the Temple receiving a message from an angel that will change history.

Today’s Scripture:

📖 Leviticus 26:14–27:34 – The final warnings of the Law, the promise of restoration for a repentant heart, and the regulations for dedicated gifts and tithes. This marks the end of our journey through the "Manual of Holiness."

📖 Luke 1:1–25 – Luke introduces his Gospel as a carefully researched account, starting with the angel Gabriel appearing to Zechariah to announce the birth of John the Baptist.

📖 Proverbs 7:1–5 – A father’s plea to prize wisdom above all else: "Write them on the tablet of your heart."
