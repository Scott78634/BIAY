Liberty and Life: The Year of Jubilee & The Resurrection of Jesus (Day 71)

What does true restoration look like? Today in Leviticus, we explore the radical concept of the "Year of Jubilee"—a season of debt cancellation and freedom that pointed toward a future hope. That hope is realized in our New Testament reading as we reach the empty tomb. Death is defeated, the stone is rolled away, and Jesus is Risen, commissioning His followers to take this message of liberty to the whole world.

Today’s Scripture:

📖 Leviticus 25–26:13 – The laws of the Sabbatical Year and the Year of Jubilee. These passages outline God's heart for social justice, the redemption of property, and the promise of blessing for obedience.

📖 Mark 16 – The climax of the Gospel: the women find the empty tomb, the angel announces the Resurrection, and Jesus appears to His disciples before ascending to the right hand of God.

📖 Psalm 33:1–11 – A call to the righteous to "rejoice in the Lord," celebrating the steadfast love of the One whose counsel stands forever.
