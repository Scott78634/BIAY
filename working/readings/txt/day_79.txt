Thematic: Following the Cloud & The Voice in the Wilderness: Numbers 9–11 and Luke 3 (Day 79)

How do you know when it’s time to move? Today in Numbers, we see Israel living by a simple but profound rule: when the Cloud moves, they move; when it stays, they stay. We hear the blast of the silver trumpets signaling the start of their journey from Sinai. But as we move into the New Testament, we hear a different kind of "trumpet blast"—the fiery preaching of John the Baptist, calling a nation to repentance and witnessing the Spirit descend upon Jesus at His baptism.

Today’s Scripture:

📖 Numbers 9:15–11:3 – The Cloud and Fire guide the camp, the two silver trumpets are fashioned to summon the congregation, and the march finally begins. However, the journey is met with immediate "complaining in the hearing of the Lord" at Taberah.

📖 Luke 3:1–22 – John the Baptist begins his ministry in the wilderness, challenging the religious and the common people alike. The episode culminates in the baptism of Jesus and the Father’s declaration: "You are my beloved Son; with you I am well pleased."

📖 Psalm 35:19–28 – A prayer for God to vindicate the righteous against those who "winking with the eye" plot mischief, ending with a commitment to speak of God's righteousness all day long.
