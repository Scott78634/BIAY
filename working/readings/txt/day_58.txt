A Living Sacrifice: Starting Leviticus & Jesus on True Greatness (Day 58 / Feb 27)

How do we approach a Holy God? Today we begin the book of Leviticus, exploring the Burnt, Grain, and Peace offerings that set the stage for understanding sacrifice and worship. In the New Testament, Jesus continues to turn the world's values upside down, teaching His disciples that true greatness is found in service and addressing the sanctity of marriage.

Today’s Scripture:

📖 Leviticus 1–3 – We dive into the "Manual for Worship," covering the Burnt Offering (devotion), the Grain Offering (tribute), and the Peace Offering (fellowship).

📖 Mark 9:33–10:12 – Jesus defines greatness through the heart of a child, warns against stumbling blocks, and answers tough questions about divorce.

📖 Psalm 27:1–6 – A powerful declaration of confidence: "The Lord is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear?"
