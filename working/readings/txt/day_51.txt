Entering the Holy Place: The Tabernacle, Jairus’ Daughter, & The King of Glory (Day 51 / Feb 20)


How do we approach a Holy God? Today we look at the intricate design of the Bronze Altar, the sacred garments of the High Priest, and two incredible miracles of faith as Jesus brings hope to the hopeless.

Today’s Scripture:

📖 Exodus 27–28 – Discover the blueprints for the Altar and the symbolic "holy garments" designed for Aaron’s priesthood.

📖 Mark 5:21–6:6 – Jesus heals a woman’s long-term suffering and raises a young girl from the dead, before facing unbelief in His hometown.

📖 Psalm 24 – A powerful declaration of who may stand in God's holy place: "Lift up your heads, O gates!"
