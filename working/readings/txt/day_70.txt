The Festivals of Israel & The Darkness at Golgotha | Day 70

How does God mark time and memory? Today in Leviticus, we explore the appointed feasts of the Lord—from Passover to Tabernacles—which served as a rhythm of worship for Israel. These shadows of things to come meet their reality in our New Testament reading, as we witness the darkness over the land, the cry of the Savior, and the quiet burial of Jesus in a tomb.

Today’s Scripture:

📖 Leviticus 23–24 – A masterclass in the biblical calendar, detailing the Sabbath and the seven annual feasts, followed by the laws for the Tabernacle lamps, the Bread of the Presence, and the gravity of blasphemy.

📖 Mark 15:33–47 – The final moments of Jesus on the cross, the tearing of the Temple veil, and the courage of Joseph of Arimathea as he asks for the body of Jesus to give Him a proper burial.

📖 Psalm 32 – A beautiful meditation on the joy of forgiveness: "Blessed is the one whose transgression is forgiven, whose sin is covered."
