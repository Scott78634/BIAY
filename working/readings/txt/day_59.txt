Atonement & The Rich Young Ruler: Leviticus 4–5 and the Cost of the Kingdom (Day 59 \ Feb 28)

Is it possible to be "good enough" for God? Today’s reading in Leviticus breaks down the Sin Offering, showing that even unintentional sins require a remedy to restore our relationship with a Holy God. This sets the stage for our reading in Mark, where Jesus meets a wealthy young man who thinks he has kept all the laws, only to realize that the one thing he lacks is the one thing he cannot give up.

Today’s Scripture:

📖 Leviticus 4–5:13 – The procedures for the Sin Offering and the Guilt Offering, emphasizing that God provides a way for cleansing, regardless of a person’s status or wealth.

📖 Mark 10:13–31 – Jesus blesses the children, challenges the Rich Young Ruler, and reminds His disciples that "all things are possible with God."

📖 Psalm 27:7–14 – A beautiful exhortation to "wait for the Lord" and take courage, even when the heart feels overwhelmed.
