Authority and Obedience: Korah’s Rebellion & The Miraculous Catch (Day 82)


Who has the right to lead? Today in Numbers, we see a dangerous challenge to Moses’ leadership as Korah and his followers rebel, claiming a holiness they hadn't been assigned—leading to a terrifying display of God's judgment. In the Gospel of Luke, we see the true nature of divine authority: Jesus heals Peter’s mother-in-law, cleanses a leper, and demonstrates His power over nature with a miraculous catch of fish that compels Peter, James, and John to leave everything and follow Him.

Today’s Scripture:

📖 Numbers 15–16:35 – Laws regarding intentional vs. unintentional sins and the reminder of the tassels on garments lead into the Rebellion of Korah. The ground literally opens up to swallow those who sought to seize the priesthood for themselves.

📖 Luke 4:38–5:16 – Jesus heals many in Capernaum before moving to the Lake of Gennesaret. After a miraculous catch of fish, Peter realizes his own sinfulness in the presence of the Holy One, yet Jesus calls him to become a "fisher of men."

📖 Psalm 37:1–9 – A timeless exhortation for the frustrated soul: "Fret not yourself because of evildoers... Trust in the Lord, and do good."
