Reparation and Ransom: Leviticus 5–7 and the Blind Man’s Faith (Day 60 / March 1 or Feb 29)

What does it cost to make things right? Today in Leviticus, we explore the "Guilt Offering," which focuses on making restitution when we’ve wronged God or others. This theme of "paying the price" reaches its fulfillment in our New Testament reading, where Jesus explains that He did not come to be served, but to give His life as a ransom for many.

Today’s Scripture:

📖 Leviticus 5:14–7:10 – The laws of the Guilt Offering and the specific instructions for the priests regarding the grain and sin offerings. It’s a lesson in the importance of restitution and holiness.

📖 Mark 10:32–52 – Jesus predicts His death for the third time, corrects James and John’s request for power, and heals blind Bartimaeus on the road to Jericho.

📖 Proverbs 6:12–19 – A sobering look at the "seven things the Lord hates," focusing on the heart and actions that create discord.
