Ordained for Service: The First Priests & The Triumphal Entry (Day 61 / March 2 or 1)


What does it look like to be "set apart" for God? Today in Leviticus, we move from the rules of the offerings to the actual ordination of Aaron and his sons. This sacred installation of the priesthood provides a striking backdrop to our reading in Mark, where Jesus enters Jerusalem as the long-awaited King, immediately asserting His authority by cleansing the Temple.

Today’s Scripture:

📖 Leviticus 7:11–8:36 – The specific laws for the Peace Offering and the detailed, seven-day ordination ceremony where Moses anoints the Tabernacle and the priests.

📖 Mark 11:1–26 – Jesus rides into Jerusalem on a colt, curses the fig tree, clears the money changers out of the Temple, and is questioned by the religious leaders.

📖 Psalm 28 – A plea for God to hear the voice of prayer, ending with a joyful declaration that the Lord is the saving refuge of His anointed.
